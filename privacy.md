Privacy Policy

Data Access

Froopo Drive accesses Google Drive files through Google OAuth authorization and Rclone for personal file management and synchronization.

Data Usage

Google Drive data is accessed to perform tasks authorized by the account owner. Depending on the task, selected file contents may be processed by the configured AI service.

Data Storage and Security

Files and authorization credentials may be stored on the owner's self-hosted server. Authorization credentials must not be publicly disclosed.

Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use and does not sell Google user data.

Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke the application's access at any time through Google Account security settings.
